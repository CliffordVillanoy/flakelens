Design note

Toolbar: search in the log with a live count (2 of 5, role status), previous and next match, Wrap lines switch, Copy line. Line numbers are muted and not selectable. Evidence lines carry a left accent bar and the info fill, and the matched words are underlined with dots, so none of it depends on colour. The current line from a chip jump gets a focus ring. A line over 2,000 characters is cut with Show more. A 5 MB log draws at most 150 rows at a time.

3. Evidence chips

Design note

A chip quotes one evidence line and says where it came from. Quoted from the log means the line was found in the input at that line number. Not found in the log means the engine could not find the text, so treat it as a hint only and it is not highlighted. Click or Enter jumps to the line in the viewer and moves focus there. The chip is a button with the full quote as its accessible name.

Design note

Plain words first (the sentence), the numbers second (bars with counts and rates), the method last (a closed details block). Strength is a word: Strong, Moderate, Weak. It is never a bare p-value. Sentences say 'fails more often on', never 'is caused by'. Not enough data and missing data are calm explanations with the exact threshold and what to do. They never show a rate as a finding.
